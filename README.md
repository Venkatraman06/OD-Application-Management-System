# Online OD Application Management System

A full-stack **Online On-Duty (OD) Application Management System** designed to digitize and simplify the traditional paper-based OD process used in colleges.

The system provides a **4-level role-based access workflow** for Students, Staff, HODs, and Admins, allowing OD applications, approvals, notifications, reports, calendars, and student management to be handled digitally from a single platform.

## Why This Project?

In many colleges, the OD process is still handled using paper forms and physical signatures. Students have to write OD requests, visit departments, wait for staff and HOD approvals, and collect signed documents.

This system converts that traditional process into a digital workflow where students can apply for OD online, staff can review and approve/reject requests, HODs can provide the final decision, and administrators can manage the overall system.

---

## Roles & Responsibilities

### Student

- Apply for Individual OD
- Apply for Group OD
- Upload supporting documents/proofs
- View applied OD status
- View OD history
- Search and filter OD records
- Print approved OD reports
- Access the academic calendar relevant to their class, year, and section
- Receive OD-related notifications

### Staff

- View OD requests submitted by assigned students
- Review complete OD details
- Approve or reject OD requests
- Edit OD date/time where applicable
- Receive email notifications for new OD requests
- Manage calendar entries for their assigned class/year/section
- Add Holiday and Examination dates
- View relevant academic calendar information
- Access OD reports and analytics

### HOD

- Receive OD requests approved by Staff
- Review OD details
- Provide the final approval or rejection
- Monitor OD activity and analytics
- Search and filter OD reports
- View department-level academic calendar information
- Add/edit Holiday and Examination dates
- Monitor calendar changes made by Staff
- Filter calendar information by Year and Section
- View combined calendar information for the department

### Admin

- Manage Student accounts/data
- Manage Staff accounts/data
- Manage HOD accounts/data
- Manage system-level configuration
- Configure OD reference number prefixes
- Configure calendar day-type colors
- Manage academic year/semester information
- Handle requests for undoing incorrect Staff/HOD approval or rejection actions
- Monitor and manage system data

---

## OD Approval Workflow

Student → Apply Individual/Group OD → Staff Review → Approve/Reject → HOD Review → Final Approve/Reject → Student Receives Final Status
